Questions

Why not just use Notes or Reminders?

You don’t have to choose a list first. Say it, and Speak It decides whether it’s something to do or something to keep.

What if it gets something wrong?

Your original words are always kept. Tap an item to fix its title, date or reminder.

What happens after 6 captures?

The 6 free captures are once, not monthly. After that, Pro adds unlimited captures. Everything you saved stays.

Does it work without signal?

Your saved items, search and checking things off all work offline. Turning speech into text may need a connection.
